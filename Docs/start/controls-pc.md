# Controls for PC (mouse and keyboard)

> *AI-reviewed content: may change at any time and should not be fully trusted. See the [disclaimer](disclaimer.md).*

> **In simple terms.** Move with **A** and **D**. Attack with the **mouse**: **left button shoots**, **right button
> uses the melee swing**. Scroll the wheel to change weapon. **E** enters or leaves a building. **Esc** pauses.

The PC version treats your mouse like a finger on the original touch screen, so every on-screen button can also be
clicked.

## City Grind (the main game)

| Action | Mouse / keyboard | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Walk left / right | **A** / **D** | The camera follows you sideways. |
| Shoot | **Left mouse button** or **K** | Weapons C and D, and the F2 and F3 modes, are automatic: hold the button to keep firing. Weapons A, B, E and F1 fire once per press. |
| Melee swing | **Right mouse button** or **J** | Press again during a swing and keep holding until it ends and the next swing starts by itself, as on the original. Holding only the first press does not repeat. |
| Next weapon | **Mouse wheel** (either direction) or **L** | The wheel changes weapon once per scroll gesture. |
| Enter / leave a building | **E**, or click the door icon | The door icon appears when you stand close to the door. It activates when you *release* the click on it. |
| Pause | **Esc**, or click the pause button | **Esc** also goes Back in menus and closes the on-screen keyboard first. |
| Fullscreen on/off | **F11** or **Alt + Enter** | Works in menus and in play. |

## Menus and shop

Use the mouse for everything: click buttons, click items to buy, click Back. **Esc** acts as the Back button.

*This table was checked against the input code, not newly tested on a device.*

## Why the controls behave a little oddly

These come from the original game and are kept on purpose (details in
[How it works: combat controls](../how-it-works/05-combat-controls.md)):

- The game remembers whether a button is **held**, not taps. A quick tap during a swing is ignored.
- Switching weapon by hand cancels a held shoot or melee.
- Pausing cancels held buttons.

## Debug keys (off by default)

Extra keys for testing exist behind a switch in *Options → Debug Tools*. They are not part of normal play and are
documented in the tools section, not here.

## Android and touch

On phones and tablets use the on-screen controls exactly as on the original; the PC keys above do not apply.
